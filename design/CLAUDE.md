# Project notes

- Rise & Flour is now a CAFÉ only. It was a bakery, then a bakery & café, now just a café. Never describe it as a bakery in new copy — "café", "the kitchen", "the counter" are right. Food is the focus.
- No ecommerce anywhere. No shop, no baskets, no checkout. Reservations/enquiries happen by email or in person.
- Type rules: Core Magic Bold ONLY for logo and product names (e.g. menu dish names). Headings, nav, caps labels: Lemon Milk — substituted with Montserrat, always caps, tracked (.09em headings, .22em labels). Body: Garamond — substituted with EB Garamond; italic for quotes/highlights.
- The user can supply real photography for heroes and blocks — image slots are drag-and-drop placeholders for them.
- Visual reference the user likes: lefarfallecharleston.com — full-page image hero with centred logo and overlaid nav, quiet image+text blocks, large image carousel.
